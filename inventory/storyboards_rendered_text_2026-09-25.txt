THÈRIA · AR ANIMATION PROGRAMME · HOSPITALITY SLATE
EIGHT HOTELS, EIGHT AWAKENINGS

Beat-by-beat storyboards for eight scan-to-animate films, 10–15 seconds each. Every film opens on a still the guest already sees — a crest, a sculpture, a mark — and ends back where it began, so the loop is seamless and the artwork is never left behind. Frames below are composited from supplied reference; anything marked to source still needs art.

8 FILMS
10–15 SEC · SEAMLESS LOOP
TRIGGER: PRINTED MARK / QR
MASTERS: 1:1 + 9:16 + 16:9
01
GIRAFFE MANOR
NAIROBI, KENYA · THE HOUSE THAT COMES TO THE WINDOW
TRT 12.0S
6 BEATS

Sound: dawn birdsong, a single hoof-fall on wet grass, porcelain touching porcelain on the last beat. No music sting until 0:08.

0:00 – 0:02
BEAT 01 · HOLD
The Sentinel

Single giraffe on flat ivory, dead centre, full body. Nothing else in frame. He blinks once and the ear flicks — the only proof this is a film.

↳ Rise 14px + fade 700ms on entry, then hold. Camera locked.

GIRAFFE MANOR
0:02 – 0:04
BEAT 02 · REVEAL
Mark of the House

The Giraffe Manor wordmark resolves on the ground line, directly under his hooves — as though he is standing on the name.

↳ Letter-spacing eases from .55em to .34em over 900ms; rule draws out from centre.

GIRAFFE MANOR
0:04 – 0:06
BEAT 03 · ENTRANCE
Second Arrival

A second giraffe walks in from frame right, slightly smaller, facing back toward the first. Four unhurried strides. The wordmark begins to give way.

↳ Walk cycle at 0.6× real speed; wordmark to 35% opacity by 0:06.

0:06 – 0:08
BEAT 04 · WORLD-BUILD
The House Appears

The wordmark clears and the ivory dissolves into lawn: the ivy-clad manor rises behind them, soft-focus at first, then settling to full clarity.

↳ Cross-dissolve 800ms + 6→0px blur ramp. Giraffes stay pin-sharp throughout.

0:08 – 0:10
BEAT 05 · TURN
Both Turn Away

In unison, both giraffes rotate away from camera and walk toward the house, necks swinging. We follow at their shoulder.

↳ Slow 8% push-in on the manor; giraffes scale down 12% as they recede.

GIRAFFE MANOR
0:10 – 0:12
BEAT 06 · PAYOFF
Heads Through the Window

Cut inside: two heads lean through the breakfast window over a laid table. Hold two seconds. The wordmark re-stamps in ivory over the scrim, and the film cuts back to Beat 01.

↳ Loop point: match-cut on the wordmark, not the image.

TO SOURCE
Vector or high-res Giraffe Manor wordmark (supplied file is 46px), a second giraffe cut-out at a different pose, and a clean manor plate without guests for the Beat 04 reveal.

The lake floods in behind them — mountains, cypress, the villa on its terrace. All three break the surface together, water sheeting off gold.

05
NORTH ISLAND
SEYCHELLES · WHITE, BLUE, WHITE
TRT 13.0S
6 BEATS

Sound: underwater pressure on the blue beats, surf and shell on the sand beat, then silence on the return to white. The whole film is one held breath.

0:00 – 0:02
BEAT 01 · HOLD
Gold on White

The full mark: four gold turtles scattered around the north island wordmark, dead still on white. Reads as an engraved plaque.

↳ Two seconds of absolute stillness. Sell it as a photograph before it moves.

0:02 – 0:04
BEAT 02 · TRANSFORM
The White Becomes Water

Caustics creep in from the edges of frame until the white ground is turquoise shallows. The turtles and the wordmark do not move — the world moves under them.

↳ Radial wipe from the four corners, 1.4s. Add refraction distortion to the wordmark as the water passes over it.

0:04 – 0:07
BEAT 03 · ANIMATE
They Begin to Swim

Front flippers sweep; the four turtles drift out of their logo positions and into a loose formation, the big one leading. Wakes trail behind them.

↳ Break formation gradually — the shape of the logo should survive to about 0:05.5.

0:07 – 0:10
BEAT 04 · LANDFALL
Onto the Sand

Camera surfaces and swings level: the bay, the granite, the lodge under the palms. The turtles haul up onto white sand, leaving drag tracks.

↳ Water-line crossing needs real weight — slow each turtle 30% as it takes the sand.

0:10 – 0:12
BEAT 05 · RETURN
Back to Sea

They turn and go back down the beach into the shallows. The tracks stay behind them in the sand for a beat, then a wave takes them.

↳ Mirror the plate so the exit reads opposite the entrance.

0:12 – 0:13
BEAT 06 · SIGN-OFF
Blue, Then White

Blue drains out of frame the way it came in; the four turtles settle back into their exact logo positions as the ground returns to white. Frame 1 and last frame are identical.

↳ Match-frame check required — this loop has to be invisible.

TO SOURCE
Each gold turtle as its own layer (or the 3D source), and a clean beach plate without turtles for the landfall composite.

TRT 12.0S
6 BEATS

Sound: lemur chatter, palm rustle, one soft catch. The only comic film in the slate — keep it dry, never cartoonish.

0:00 – 0:02
BEAT 01 · HOLD
Two Lemurs, Re-staged

The sage lockup, but the lemurs are repositioned for the gag: left one perched on the M, right one hanging by its feet from the final A, both facing centre so the throw reads across the word.

↳ Tail sway and a single head-turn. Nothing else.

0:02 – 0:04
BEAT 02 · SETUP
The Mango

The hanging lemur produces a mango from nowhere, weighs it, and cocks its arm. A beat of deliberation.

↳ Anticipation hold of 12 frames before the throw — all the comedy is in the pause.

0:04 – 0:05.5
BEAT 03 · ACTION
Across the Wordmark

The mango arcs over the letterforms and is caught one-handed by the lemur on the M, which does not otherwise move.

↳ The arc must clear the cap-height and pass in front of the type, never behind it.

0:05.5 – 0:08
BEAT 04 · WORLD-BUILD
The Sanctuary Behind

The sage field deepens into a real horizon: the villas, the pools, the sandbar at low sun. The wordmark fades as the place takes over.

↳ Colour-match the plate's greens back toward the sage brand ground so the change feels like focus, not a cut.

0:08 – 0:10
BEAT 05 · PAYOFF
Up in the Palms

The lemurs are now in the palms above the resort, still passing the mango, the throw continuing uninterrupted through the change of world.

↳ Inset shows the lemur reference for the palm pose. Keep the throw rhythm identical to Beat 03.

0:10 – 0:12
BEAT 06 · SIGN-OFF
Back to the Mark

The resort dissolves back to flat sage; the palms become letterforms again and the two lemurs settle into their opening positions. The mango is gone. Nobody mentions it.

↳ Loop point. Consider one frame of the mango still mid-air as an easter egg on the third pass.

TO SOURCE
Lemur cut-outs in throw and catch poses (supplied stills are static), a mango asset, and the Miavana wordmark as vector so the lemurs can be re-positioned on the letterforms.

22 CLUB
THE NUMERALS BECOME THE DECKS
TRT 12.0S
6 BEATS

Sound: low, slow, sexy techno — one bar in, one bar out. The beat drops on the spotlight (Beat 04) and everything before it is filtered.

0:00 – 0:02
BEAT 01 · HOLD
The Mark, Cold

The red 22 club mark on ivory, centred, static. Filtered music already running under it, as if heard through a door.

↳ No movement at all for two seconds. The stillness sets up the flip.

0:02 – 0:04
BEAT 02 · TURN
The 22 Lies Down

Both numerals rotate onto their backs — a slow 90° fall, as if tipped. The "club" script stays upright and slides down out of the way.

↳ Ease-out with a small overshoot and settle. Ground goes to ink as they land.

0:04 – 0:06
BEAT 03 · TRANSFORM
Twin Decks

The two fallen numerals become platters: each curl of the 2 unrolls into a record, red label at centre, tonearm swinging in from the right. Both start spinning.

↳ Spin locks to the track's BPM. One deck runs a half-beat behind the other.

0:06 – 0:09
BEAT 04 · DROP
Spotlight

A hard spot snaps on from above, throwing a cone of haze across both decks. It sweeps left to right and back, catching the grooves.

↳ Snap on with the beat — one frame, no fade. Specular streak travels the vinyl on each pass.

0:09 – 0:11
BEAT 05 · RETURN
Upright Again

The records slow, the grooves flatten back into strokes, and the numerals rotate upright — still lit, still red, still moving slightly.

↳ Reverse the Beat 02 curve exactly, 20% faster. Track filters back down as they rise.

0:11 – 0:12
BEAT 06 · SIGN-OFF
Lights Out

Spotlight cuts, the ink ground lifts back to ivory, and the mark sits exactly as it began. The music keeps going one bar past the picture.

↳ Loop point. Audio tail is the only thing that breaks the seam — intentional.

TO SOURCE
Vector 22 club mark with the numerals and script on separate paths, a turntable/vinyl reference for the platter build, and the licensed techno bed.
